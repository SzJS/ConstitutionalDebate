However, Bob's objection is that the sentence's implication, in the context of the argument, is misleading because it suggests that the downsides of CT scans (radiation, cost, time) are relevant to choosing amylase testing first, when in fact, the critical issue is whether amylase testing alone can confirm or rule out a clinically significant leak.

> Therefore, the text under review, sentence 6, is not flawed because it is factually accurate and does not contain a misleading implication that would make it wrong, illogical, or misleading in the context provided.
